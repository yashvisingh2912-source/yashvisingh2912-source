## Projects

### Nearby Wells Intelligence System (NWIS)
Team project. I led the ML and RAG components.

Drilling decisions depend on risks buried in offset-well data and long technical documents. NWIS surfaces those risks and makes them queryable.

- ML pipeline that predicts operational drilling risk, with SHAP explanations showing which factors drove each prediction
- RAG pipeline that retrieves from drilling documents and generates answers grounded in the retrieved context
- Integrated the ML and RAG services into the larger application with teammates

**Stack:** Python, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, FastAPI, Qdrant, Sentence Transformers, LLMs
[Repository](https://github.com/vidhiii1711/eRTMAC-NWIS)

### Know Your PCOS Risk Early
Individual project.

PCOS often goes undetected, and in screening a missed case costs more than a false alarm. This app estimates risk from patient features and explains the result.

- Compared classification models and optimized for recall to reduce missed positives
- SHAP-based explanations so users can see which factors influence their result
- Two workflows: symptom-based and clinical-analysis

**Stack:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, SHAP, Streamlit
[Repository](https://github.com/yashvisingh2912-source/PCOS-Risk-Predictor)
